Shipping

We ship orders within 1 business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $7.99, or is free on orders over $99. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. Store pickup is free; we email you when your order is ready.

You get an email when your order ships, with the carrier and tracking number. If a package is lost or arrives damaged, email us within 7 days of the delivery date and we'll replace it.

Returns and refunds

You can return any device within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. It must be in like-new condition, with its box and all accessories. Email us with your order number to start a return.

We cover return shipping if the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one; otherwise return shipping is paid by you. Refunds are issued within 5 business days of receiving the return and can take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Warranty

Every device has a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. If it fails in normal use, we repair or replace it at no cost. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage beyond the device's water rating, or unauthorised repairs.

Payment

You pay when your order is delivered or picked up, by card or cash. Discount codes are applied to the price of the items, before shipping and tax. Prices include an estimate of sales tax at checkout; the final amount is confirmed in your order email.

Privacy

We use your name, email, phone number and address only to process, deliver and support your order and to send you order updates. We do not sell or share your details with anyone except the carrier delivering your order. Email us to have your details deleted.

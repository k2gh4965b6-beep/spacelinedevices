Store policies

Shipping

Orders ship within 1 business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $7.99, or is free on orders over $99. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. You get an email with tracking when your order ships.

Returns and refunds

Return any device within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. It must be in like-new condition with its box and accessories. Email us with your order number to start a return. We cover return shipping if the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one.

Warranty

Every device has a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. If it fails in normal use, we repair or replace it at no cost. Accidental and liquid damage beyond the device's water rating are not covered.

Payment

Pay online with Apple Pay or card when you place your order, through our secure Stripe checkout. Or choose pay on delivery and pay by card or cash when it arrives.

Privacy

We use your name, email, phone number and address only to process, deliver and support your order. We don't sell or share your details with anyone except the carrier delivering your order.
